Modern Ad Serving Architecture in High-Traffic Systems

In high-performance ad networks, latency is measured in milliseconds. When a publisher page requests an ad slot, the ad server must filter eligible campaigns, verify frequency caps, enforce budget limits, and respond with an optimal creative instantly.

Redis caching allows candidates to be queried with sub-millisecond overhead, and atomic counters prevent overselling impressions. Click tracking through transparent redirects provides fraud resistance and real-time CTR attribution.
